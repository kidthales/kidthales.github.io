## Overview

Spiderex is an interactive guitar fretboard permutational spider-exercise trainer designed to build chromatic finger independence, synchronization, and fretboard dexterity. First released in May 2025 (PR #19) and rebuilt in November 2026 as a zero-CLS custom-element island on `next`.

## Spider Mechanics

Traditional chromatic spider exercises follow predictable sequential patterns (`1-2-3-4` across strings). Spiderex generates non-repeating mathematical permutations of chromatic fingerings across configurable string groupings, breaking muscle-memory stagnation and forcing deliberate finger placement.
